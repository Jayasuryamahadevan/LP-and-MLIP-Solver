Verdict: HiGHS is faster on LP in aggregate (mature, decades-tuned simplex) and stronger on 2 of 5 MILP instances (deeper cut/heuristic arsenal). This repo's solver matches HiGHS's LP objectives on 89/90 instances, proves the same infeasibility faster on the one case tested, and found a strictly better MILP incumbent on one instance. No aggregate superiority claim is made for either LP speed or MILP certification — see the doc for the full, unhedged account, including the one objective disagreement (e226) and its diagnosed cause.
